Everything a fuel log needs — nothing it doesn't

Built around how bike and car owners actually track their vehicles, not a generic form builder.

Fuel tracking

Log every fill-up with litres, cost, odometer and full-tank flag. Odometer rollback protection keeps your history clean even when you backfill old entries.

Mileage & cost analytics

Tank-to-tank km/L calculated automatically, with trend lines, cost-per-km, and smart insights on your best and average mileage.

Service history

Track oil changes, tyre work and any other service by type, with a full odometer-based history and cost breakdown per vehicle.

Document vault

Insurance, RC book, PUC certificates and warranties in one place, with colour-coded expiry status and a dashboard banner before anything lapses.

CSV export

Download your fuel history, service history or mileage report as CSV any time — UTF-8 BOM included so Excel opens it cleanly.

Install as an app

PitLog is a full PWA — install it on Android, iOS or desktop, with an offline fallback page and quick shortcuts to add fuel or service.
